Core flow

SuperAdmin → Firebase → Public Website

You add/edit a trek once from the SuperAdmin panel, and the public website automatically displays the updated information.

Customer flow
Visitor
 ↓
Explore Trek/Tour/Vehicle
 ↓
View Complete Details
 ↓
Send Enquiry
 ↓
Firebase stores enquiry
 ↓
You receive notification
 ↓
Call / WhatsApp customer
 ↓
Manually finalize booking
Tech stack

Frontend: Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind
Database: Firebase Firestore
Admin Login: Firebase Authentication
Images: Cloudinary
Hosting: Vercel
Booking: Enquiry-based, no direct online booking initially
